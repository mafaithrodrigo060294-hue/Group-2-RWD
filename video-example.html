Responsive Video Studio

Videos should adapt to the available screen space without overflowing their containers. Responsive video design helps maintain proper proportions and provides a consistent viewing experience across different devices.

Understanding Responsive Video

The width property allows a video to scale according to its parent container, while height: auto preserves its original aspect ratio. The controls attribute enables built-in playback controls, and playsinline allows inline playback on supported mobile browsers.
