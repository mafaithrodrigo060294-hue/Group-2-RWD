Lesson // Video controls

Responsive Videos

Responsive video keeps its aspect ratio and fits the available width without overflowing its container.

Making videos responsive

Video should scale with the page and preserve its aspect ratio. The simplest approach is to give the video a maximum width of 100% and an automatic height.

Responsive embedded video

For an iframe such as a video embed, use a wrapper with an aspect ratio. The iframe fills the wrapper.

Use the HTML controls attribute when users need playback controls. Use muted and loop only when they make sense for the experience. Avoid autoplay with sound.
